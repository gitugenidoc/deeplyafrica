## Introduction

The CAF Confederation Cup is difficult to predict because squad depth, travel and knockout timing can quickly change a team's season.

## Context

The tournament often brings together clubs with different budgets, domestic pressures and continental experience.

The best teams manage defensive transitions and avoid emotional overexposure away from home.

## What It Means

The Confederation Cup remains a powerful stage for clubs outside the usual continental spotlight.
