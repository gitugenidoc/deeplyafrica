# DeeplyAfrica Media Kit

## About DeeplyAfrica

DeeplyAfrica is a Morocco-based digital sports media platform focused on African football.

## Mission

To cover African football with original reporting, match analysis, visual storytelling and football intelligence.

## Editorial Focus

CAF competitions, AFCON, WAFCON, CAF Women's Champions League, Botola Pro Morocco, African teams in FIFA competitions and African talents.

## Partnership Opportunities

Sponsored content, event coverage, tournament visibility, club storytelling, player branding, social media campaigns, visual design, football intelligence reports and newsletter sponsorship.
