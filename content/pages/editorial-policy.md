# Editorial Policy

DeeplyAfrica publishes original football content, including match previews, reports, player profiles, tactical analysis, interviews and visual storytelling. When information from official sources is used, it is clearly attributed. DeeplyAfrica does not operate as a content aggregator.

## Original Reporting

DeeplyAfrica prioritizes original editorial work, football context and analysis beyond the scoreline.

## Source Attribution

Official information from competitions, clubs, federations and FIFA sources must be attributed clearly.

## Corrections Policy

Corrections are reviewed and applied when verified information changes.

## Image and Media Policy

Images and media must be original, licensed, credited or used with a clear rights context.

## Editorial Independence

Commercial relationships do not determine editorial conclusions.
